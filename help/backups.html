Backups (admins)

How the wiki is backed up, and how to restore it.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

Admins find backups in Configuration → Backup and restore: when they run, where they go, how many are kept, and Restore to go back to one. Keep at least one backup on another drive or PC.
