Bookmarks, watching and notifications

Keep pages at hand, hear when they change, and choose what reaches your bell.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

  • Bookmark at the top of a page adds it to your Bookmarks (the star in the top bar).
  • Watch tells you when the page (or its whole space) changes or gets a comment. Pages you make or edit are watched for you.
  • The bell lists what's new: edits to pages you watch, replies, pages shared with you, and @mentions of you.
  • Your account → Notifications chooses what you hear about, in the bell, the browser and on the wiki's own PC.
