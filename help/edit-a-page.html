Edit a page

The editor's toolbar, shortcuts, drafts, and the note that goes into the page's history.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

Click Edit at the top of the page. The toolbar has paragraph styles, bold and italic, colors, lists and checklists, alignment, layouts, links, pictures and files, tables and the Insert menu (panels, dates, statuses, a table of contents, and more).

Typing shortcuts

  • [ links to a page, @ mentions a person, ! adds one of the page's pictures, { opens the Insert menu.
  • Markdown and Confluence's autoformat work: # or h1. for a heading, *bold*, [] for a checkbox.
  • Ctrl+F finds and replaces; Ctrl+/ lists every shortcut.

Saving

Your draft is kept as you type, so closing the tab loses nothing. Under the text, What did you change? leaves a note in the page's history, and unchecking Notify watchers keeps a small fix quiet. Preview and View changes show the result before you Save.
