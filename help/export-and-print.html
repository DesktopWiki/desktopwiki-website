Export, print and present

Save a page as PDF, Word or Markdown, show it as slides, or keep it for offline reading.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

A page's ⋯ menu has:

  • Export: PDF (print or save), Word document, Markdown, or the page with the pages under it as a .zip.
  • Present: the page full screen as slides, a new slide at each big heading.
  • Keep offline: a copy on this device to read with no connection.

Space tools → Export space exports a whole space.
