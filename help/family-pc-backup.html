Back up the family's PCs (admins)

DesktopWiki Helper on each family PC backs up its people's folders to the wiki's PC, with versions, restore and alerts.

Last updated Nov 26, 2026

Open Network → Family PCs and add a PC by its name. On that PC, sign in to Windows as an administrator, open the PC's page, download DesktopWiki Helper, open it and paste the setup code. The helper runs as a Windows service, so it backs up even when no one is signed in, and a child on a standard account can't stop it.

Choose whose folders (Desktop, Documents, Pictures and more), how often, how much room the PC may use, and how long old versions and deleted files are kept. Only changed files are sent, and each file's contents are kept once. You're told on the bell when a PC hasn't backed up in its time, when it's full, and when files were put back.

Browse and restore files shows a PC's folders as they are now or as they were on a day, and the files deleted since. Download a file or a folder (.zip), or have the helper put it back on the PC, in a new folder or where it was. The backups are on the wiki's PC only: keep that PC's own backups elsewhere too.
