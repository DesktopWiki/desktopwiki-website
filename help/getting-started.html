Getting started with DesktopWiki

What spaces and pages are, and where everything is: the top bar, the sidebar and your profile menu.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

DesktopWiki keeps your family's or team's pages on one Windows PC. Anyone on your network opens it in a browser, on a PC, a tablet or a phone.

Spaces and pages

Pages live in spaces. Everyone has a personal space of their own, and admins make shared spaces (Household, Recipes, the office) for everyone. Each space has a home page, and pages sit under it in a tree.

Finding your way

  • The top bar: Spaces, People, Create, search, and on the right your bell, Help (the ?), Configuration (admins) and your profile menu.
  • The sidebar: inside a space it shows the space's pages as a tree; elsewhere, your lists (Bookmarks, My tasks, Recently visited) and your starred spaces. The button in the upper left hides and shows it, as does Ctrl+[.
  • Your profile menu (your picture, top right): your profile, your account, Theme, and Sign out.
