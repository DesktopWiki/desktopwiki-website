Home records: papers that renew

Keep passports, licenses, insurance policies, memberships and registrations with the home inventory, and hear before each runs out.

Last updated Nov 26, 2026

Open Home inventory and choose Add an item. Pick a paper's category (Document, Insurance policy, License or permit, Membership, Registration, Subscription or contract), then fill in Renewal: its number, who issued it, whom it's for, the day it renews or runs out, how often it renews, and how early to remind whoever looks after it. Scans and receipts go in its files, as for any item, and Paid with links the bill that pays for it.

Renewals coming up at the top of the inventory lists what renews in the next 60 days. When you've renewed one, choose Renewed: one that renews every year moves on a year; one that doesn't repeat waits for its new day in Edit. The inventory's filter shows all things or all papers, and the insurance list leaves papers out.
