Import from Confluence (admins)

Bring spaces, pages, attachments, comments and permissions over from a Confluence export.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

In Confluence, export a space or the whole site as XML. Then in DesktopWiki open Configuration → Import from Confluence, choose the file, and pick the spaces to bring in. Pages, their history, attachments, comments, labels, templates and space permissions come over, and a report says what came in.

Still stuck? Write to support@desktopwiki.com.
