Keyboard shortcuts

The keys that work across the wiki and in the editor.

Last updated Nov 26, 2026

As in Confluence Cloud, when you aren't typing in a box: E edits the page, C makes a new page, Alt+Shift+Z goes to Search, Ctrl+Alt+H Home, Ctrl+Alt+G Recently visited, Ctrl+Alt+8 Bookmarks, Ctrl+Alt+O Notifications, Ctrl+Alt+V the space's home, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+B a new blog post, and Ctrl+? this list. On a page: Ctrl+Alt+C comment, Ctrl+Alt+W watch, Ctrl+Alt+S share, Ctrl+Alt+A labels, Ctrl+Alt+T attachments, Ctrl+Alt+F bookmark, Ctrl+Alt+R present, Ctrl+Shift+I details, Ctrl+] comments, Ctrl+. and Ctrl+, the next and previous comment. Ctrl+Alt+Q also goes to Search.

  • Ctrl+[ hides or shows the sidebar.
  • Esc closes a menu or the Help panel.
  • In the editor: Ctrl+0 to Ctrl+8 paragraph styles, Ctrl+F find and replace, Alt+F9 the toolbar, Ctrl+/ every editor shortcut. Typing ||Heading||Heading|| or |Cell|Cell| and Enter makes a table.
  • On a whiteboard: V select, H hand, S or N sticky note, T text, E or O ellipse, L line, F section; the arrow keys nudge what's chosen (with Shift, further), Ctrl+C, X and V copy, cut and paste it, Ctrl+Alt+D duplicates it, Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[ bring it to the front or send it to the back, Ctrl+Shift+1 fits the board and Ctrl+Shift+2 zooms to what's chosen.
  • Alt+F8 in a password box shows or hides the password.
  • Ctrl+Shift+Space on the wiki's own PC opens Quick Access to your vaults.
