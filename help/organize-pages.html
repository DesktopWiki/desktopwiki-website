Move, archive or delete a page

Change where a page sits in the tree, archive it, or move it to the trash and bring it back.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

All of these are in the page's ⋯ menu, next to Share.

  • Move puts the page (and the pages under it) under another page, in this space or another one.
  • Archive takes the page out of the tree and search but keeps it, read only, with Restore on it. A space's archived pages are in Space tools → Archived pages.
  • Move to trash deletes it. Until the trash is emptied, Space tools → Trash brings it back.
