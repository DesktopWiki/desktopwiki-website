Screen time on the kids' PCs (admins)

Daily limits and bedtimes for each Windows account on a family PC, with a lock and extra time from the wiki.

Last updated Nov 26, 2026

Screen time uses DesktopWiki Helper, so set the PC up first (see Back up the family's PCs). Then open Network → Family PCs and the PC: under Screen time, each Windows account on it has Limits and bedtimes for each day of the week: how long the PC may be used, and a bedtime until the next morning.

The helper keeps the rules on the PC itself, even when the wiki can't be reached. It counts only the time someone is signed in and the PC is unlocked, warns 10, 5 and 1 minutes ahead, then locks the PC (programs keep running). From any phone or PC, a parent can give +15 min to +2 h for today, lift bedtime tonight, or Lock now.

Give the child a standard Windows account, not an administrator's: then they can't stop the helper. If it's ever stopped while the PC is on, admins are told on the bell.
