Search the wiki

Find pages as you type, or search everything with filters.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

Start typing in the Search box in the top bar: the best matches you can see appear at once, and the arrow keys and Enter open one. Press Enter without choosing to open the search page, which searches every page, blog post, comment and attached file (scanned PDFs too) and narrows by space, type and when it changed.

Labels under a page's text gather pages on a subject: click one to see every page with it.

Still stuck? Write to support@desktopwiki.com.
