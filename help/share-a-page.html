Share a page

Send a page to people in the wiki, or make a link for someone without an account.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

Click Share at the top of the page. Copy the page's link, or pick people in the wiki with a note: they hear about it under the bell.

People who can edit the page can also make a Link for anyone, which opens the page read only without signing in, for as long as you choose.

Still stuck? Write to support@desktopwiki.com.
