Spaces

Personal and shared spaces, starring a space, and who can see and edit it.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

Everyone has a personal space. Admins make shared spaces in Spaces → Space directory → Create a shared space, choosing a kind (Blank, Documentation, Knowledge base or Team), a name and a short key that goes in its address.

  • The star beside a space's name puts it in your sidebar and the top bar's Spaces menu.
  • Space tools (the ⋯ beside the space's name in the sidebar) has its details, shortcuts, templates, permissions, trash and export.
  • Permissions says who can read, edit or manage the space. A single page can also be locked from its ⋯ → Lock.
