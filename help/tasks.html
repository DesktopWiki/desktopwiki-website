Tasks and My tasks

Give a checklist item to someone with a due date, and see everything that's yours.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

In a checklist, type @ and a name to give the item to someone, and add a date to make it due. My tasks (in the sidebar's My work) lists what's yours: overdue, today, this week, later, and the tasks you gave others. Add a task there makes one without opening a page.
