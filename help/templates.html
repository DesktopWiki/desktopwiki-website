Templates

Start pages from a template, and make templates of your own for a space or the whole wiki.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

The ⋯ beside Create in the top bar starts a page from a template: built-in ones such as Meeting notes and Decision, the space's own, and the wiki's.

A space's managers keep its templates in Space tools → Templates; admins keep the wiki's in Configuration → Global templates.

Still stuck? Write to support@desktopwiki.com.
