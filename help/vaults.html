Vaults for passwords

Keep passwords and private documents in encrypted vaults, and share a vault with family.

Last updated Nov 21, 2026

Vaults in the sidebar keep logins, cards, Wi-Fi, documents and notes, encrypted in your browser with your vault password, which is separate from signing in. Nobody can read them without it, admins included.

  • When you set up vaults, save the Emergency Kit: it's how you get back in.
  • Your Private vault is yours alone; make other vaults and add family members to share them.
  • Password health finds weak and reused passwords.
