1. Plans and licenses

DesktopWiki runs on a PC you choose and serves one wiki. How many people can sign in to that wiki depends on its plan: Free, 1 person; Family, up to 6; Team, up to 18; Business, up to 60; or the number a license we make for you names. A person is any account that can sign in, guests included; disabled accounts do not count. Every plan has every feature.

Free needs no license. Family, Team and Business each need a license: a small file, signed by us, that you enter in the wiki (Configuration > License). It names its owner, the plan, the number of people and the dates it covers, and the Software checks it on your PC, without the internet. A license is for one wiki at a time. You may move the wiki and its license to another PC.

If the wiki has more people than its plan allows, nobody already in is removed, but no one else can be added or re-enabled until it moves to a larger plan.

4. Yearly plans renew until you cancel

Family, Team and Business are sold by the year. A yearly plan renews automatically at the end of each year, and Paddle charges the payment method you used, until you cancel. Each renewal sends you a new license file covering the next year. Your license covers the year you paid for plus 14 days, so a renewal that is late does not stop your wiki.

You can cancel at any time, from the link in Paddle's receipt, at paddle.net, or by writing to us. Canceling stops the next renewal; your plan keeps working until the end of the year you paid for. If we change a plan's price, we will tell you at least 30 days before a renewal at the new price, and you can cancel before it.

To move to a larger plan, write to us and we will arrange it.

5. Family bought once

Family can also be bought once. A Family license bought once never ends: the version you have keeps working with everything, for as long as you like. It includes the updates we release during its first year. After that, you can buy a year of updates at a time; without them, the Software keeps working but does not install versions released after your updates ended. A year of updates does not renew by itself.

6. When a yearly plan ends

If a yearly plan ends (you canceled, a renewal could not be charged, or it was refunded), you keep your wiki and everything in it. The wiki becomes read-only: everyone can still sign in, read, search, print and export everything, but nobody can change it. Updates stop, and so do the extras of the desktopwiki.net names service (Away from home, Open to the internet and an address you chose); the wiki's own desktopwiki.net address keeps working. Renewing, or entering a license that covers today, makes it fully work again at once. We never delete your data or lock you out of it.

You are responsible for keeping backups of your wiki, ideally on another drive, and for checking that they work. The Software can make them for you on a schedule (Configuration > Backups).
