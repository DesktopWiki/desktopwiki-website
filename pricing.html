Questions

What am I paying for, if it runs on my PC?

Updates, the desktopwiki.net names service and its certificates, and support. Your data never comes to us: we don’t host wikis, and nothing you pay for gives us a copy.

What happens if I stop paying?

You keep your wiki. When a yearly plan ends, the wiki becomes read-only: everyone can still read, search, print and export everything. Updates stop, and so do Away from home and an address you chose; the wiki’s own desktopwiki.net address keeps working. Renew and it’s back to normal at once. We never lock you out of your data.

Who counts as a person?

Every account that can sign in, guests too. Disabled accounts don’t count. If you outgrow your plan, nobody is removed: you just can’t add another person until you move up.

Why yearly, not monthly?

On small amounts, monthly billing loses much of the price to card fees. Yearly keeps it low, and adding a person costs nothing until you reach your plan’s limit (the 7th person moves you from Family to Team).

Does it need the internet to check my license?

No. Your license is a small signed file kept in the wiki, checked on the PC, so the wiki works offline.

Can I cancel, or get my money back?

Yearly plans renew until you cancel, and you can cancel any time from Paddle’s receipt; your plan runs to the end of the year you paid for. Not happy? Your money back within 30 days, for any reason. Paddle, our reseller, handles the payment, tax and refunds.
