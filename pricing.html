Questions

What am I paying for, if it runs on my PC?

Updates, the desktopwiki.net names service and its certificates, and support. Your data never comes to us: we don’t host wikis, and nothing you pay for gives us a copy.

What happens if I stop paying?

You keep your wiki. When a yearly plan ends, the wiki becomes read-only: everyone can still read, search, print and export everything. Updates and the desktopwiki.net address stop. Renew and it’s back to normal at once. We never lock you out of your data.

And if I paid once for Family?

It keeps working, fully, for as long as you like. After its year of updates you just don’t get new versions, until you add a year for $39.

Who counts as a person?

Every account that can sign in, guests too. Disabled accounts don’t count. If you outgrow your plan, nobody is removed: you just can’t add another person until you move up.

Why yearly, not monthly?

On small amounts, monthly billing loses much of the price to card fees. Yearly keeps it low, and adding a person never costs more.

Does it need the internet to check my license?

No. Your license is a small signed file kept in the wiki, checked on the PC, so the wiki works offline.
